# Brand archetype & design rationale

**Recommendation: Explorer leads (about 70%), Sage supports (about 30%).**

> An expedition guide for teams heading into AI and computer vision:
> we go further with you, and keep you level on the way.

The site's headline is that sentence in six words, built by putting the
Explorer into the middle of your existing Sage tagline:

> See clearly. **Go further.** Stay level.

---

## 1. Two names, one story

| | Equanimity Engineering AB | Expandia Labs |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The company, the logo, the legal entity | The website and practice at expandialabs.com |
| What it means | Eye + spirit level: see clearly, stay level | Expanding business, growth, mindset and breath |
| Archetype it carries | **Sage**: clarity, measurement, calm | **Explorer**: expansion, discovery, independence |

On the site, the official Equanimity logo stays in the header and footer, and
"Expandia Labs" appears as the site name (eyebrow, page title). The two halves
of the headline belong to the two names.

## 2. Why the Explorer leads

Map & Fire's field guide describes the Explorer through *independence,
bravery, freedom, self-sufficiency and nonconformity*. Your material fits it:

- **The name.** *Expandia* is expansion. *Labs* is experiment. (Even the Loopia
  account is called `experimentsoul`.)
- **The route you've taken.** Active safety in cars, automotive image sensors,
  computational microscopy, 3D volume from a phone camera, healthcare, a cigar
  shop's e-commerce, a mindfulness course with sheep. That reads as an
  expedition, not a single specialism.
- **How others describe you.** A former development manager's recommendation
  names your *explorative mindset* and how you thrived in breadth and
  complexity.
- **Independence.** A one-person AB founded in 2024, studies in Linköping and
  Seoul, work across the Øresund region.
- **What clients buy.** Someone who has already been into unfamiliar terrain
  (AI, computer vision) and can bring their team through it.

## 3. Why the Sage supports, and doesn't lead

The guide's Sage traits are *wisdom, intelligence, truth, clarity of thought,
rational decision-making and prudence*.

- Your new identity is already Sage: the eye for insight, the spirit level for
  measured balance, "Built, not drawn", "engineering you can measure".
- Teaching AI, the core of the company page, is Sage behaviour.
- Buyers in medtech, automotive and healthcare need prudence and honesty. The
  Sage is what makes the Explorer safe to hire.

But a Sage-led site would read as academic, and the momentum in *Expandia*
would be lost. So: **the Explorer sets the story, the Sage sets the voice.**

## 4. Why not the other three as the lead

| Archetype | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Creator** (imagination, originality, aesthetics) | Close runner-up | It lives in "Labs" and in what you've built, like gotteftermaten.se. But clients don't hire you for self-expression, so Creator shows up as **proof**, not personality. |
| **Hero** (courage, strength, stamina, transformation) | No | In consulting the client should be the hero. Hero's tone of struggle and victory fights *equanimity*. |
| **Outlaw** (risk-taking, provocation, rule-breaking) | A pinch only | It clashes with calm and with regulated industries. Keep a little irreverence in the copy ("Which boring task eats your week?"), nothing more. |

Map & Fire recommends one dominant archetype with one or two supporting ones,
and warns that borrowing from many blurs a brand. Two is enough here.

## 5. How the archetypes show up in the design

| Element | Explorer | Sage |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | "Go further." Contours expand outward from the bubble | "See clearly. Stay level." The logo's construction drawing, exact to the deck's geometry (W = 2.17 H, bubble = H/φ, marks = H/10) |
| Motion | Carousels step to the right, bringing the next stop on the route into focus | The bubble drifts with the pointer and always settles dead-centre; every animation can be paused |
| Words | *expeditions, terrain, route, map, further* | *clear, measure, level, honest, plain words* |
| Structure | Wide, horizontal movement; a journey in three steps (Talk, Map, Expand) | Numbered sections and ruled tables, as in the identity deck |
| Colour | Midnight sky, the far horizon | Brass lamplight, used sparingly |

## 6. Voice

**Calm, curious, precise.** Short sentences. Evidence over adjectives. Invite,
don't push.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Thirty minutes, no slides." | "Book your free strategy session today!" |
| "Where AI helps, and where it doesn't." | "AI-powered everything." |
| "A one-page route: options, effort and risks, in plain words." | "End-to-end transformation solutions." |
| "Selected expeditions." | "Our portfolio of excellence." |

Use Explorer words sparingly (one per section is plenty), and avoid war or
hustle metaphors (Hero), "disrupt" (Outlaw) and "magic" (Magician).

## 7. Visual system on the web

**Colour.** All from the identity deck, plus one web-only addition for
accessibility.

| Token | Hex | Use | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Midnight | `#0E1F33` | Text, dark grounds | 14.8:1 on Linen |
| Linen | `#F6F1E8` | Light grounds | |
| Linen 2 | `#ECE5D9` | Alternate light band | |
| Midnight 2 | `#173451` | Surfaces on Midnight (from the LinkedIn cover) | |
| Brass | `#C6A15B` | The bubble, accents on dark | 6.9:1 on Midnight |
| Deep Brass | `#A8823E` | Large accent type on light only | 3.2:1 on Linen (fails for small text) |
| **Brass Ink** *(new)* | `#85662C` | Small labels on light | 4.7:1 on Linen (passes AA) |
| Muted ink | `#4C5A6A` | Secondary text on light | 6.3:1 on Linen |

**Type.** Inter with the optical-size axis, so headlines get the Inter Display
cut automatically. Medium (500) for headlines, Regular for body, Medium caps
with 14% tracking for labels: the wordmark's own tracking.

**Motifs.** Construction lines, expanding contour rings (the pillar icons gain
one ring per pillar: Business 1, Growth 2, Mindset 3, Breath 4), little brass
bubbles as separators, and numbered labels like `01 — Expandia`.

**Motion.** One 8-second breath (4 s in, 4 s out) drives the hero. Everything
stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and a *Pause motion* control covers the
rest (WCAG 2.2.2).

**Logo.** Reverse lockup on Midnight, primary on Linen, never narrower than
140 px. Clear space is one bubble diameter. The masters stay in
`OneDrive/…/Equanimity Engineering AB/Logo/equanimity-level-final/`.

## 8. One word to confirm: *breath* or *breadth*?

The fourth pillar reads **Breath** (mindfulness, space to think), as you wrote
it. If you meant **breadth** (range of experience), it's a one-word swap plus a
new sentence. Both fit the brand; *breath* is the more distinctive.
