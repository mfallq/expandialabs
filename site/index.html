05 — About

Hi, I’m Marcus.

I founded Equanimity Engineering in 2024 to do the work I like best: helping teams see a hard problem clearly, then building their way through it, calmly.

My background spans automotive, imaging and medtech: active-safety functions, image sensors, computational microscopy and 3D computer vision. I lead with direct communication and a habit of pulling on loose ends until they’re tied, and I teach what I build so it stays with your team.

Away from the keyboard I’ve served at a Vipassana meditation centre, mentored teenagers in Malmö, and run a mindfulness course with a flock of sheep.
