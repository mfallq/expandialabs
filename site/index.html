05 — About

Hi, I’m Marcus.

I founded Equanimity Engineering in 2024 to do the work I like best: helping teams see a hard problem clearly, then building their way through it, calmly.

Ten years in, my work has moved from cameras and cars to AI. Today I build agentic AI and LLM solutions, ML and computer vision models, and the data pipelines that feed them, mostly for healthcare and medtech. Before that: camera R&D and image quality, an automotive camera SDK on NVIDIA platforms, and collision-avoidance verification in cars.

I lead with direct communication and a habit of pulling on loose ends until they’re tied, and I teach what I build so it stays with your team. Away from the keyboard: Vipassana, a year studying in Seoul, mentoring teenagers in Malmö, and a mindfulness course with a flock of sheep.
